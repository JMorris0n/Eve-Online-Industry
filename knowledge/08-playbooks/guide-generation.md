## Principle

Personal guides are derived outputs. They should not become a second source of truth for mechanics.

## Workflow

1. Define the audience, task, and decision the guide supports.
2. Select only relevant `stable` concepts and necessary `draft` caveats.
3. Copy source-backed facts into a task-specific working draft with paths preserved.
4. Apply a guide template suited to the user task.
5. Re-check all formulas, dynamic values, and citations.
6. Label guide-specific assumptions, examples, and strategic preferences.
7. Publish the guide separately from canonical concepts.

## Maintenance

When an underlying concept changes, identify dependent guides and determine whether they need regeneration or review.
