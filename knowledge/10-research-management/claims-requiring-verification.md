## Intake rule

Place imported, legacy, or model-generated claims here when they may be useful but are not yet independently supported. Do not copy them into stable mechanic documents as facts.

## Initial audit queue

- [ ] Invention probability formula, base chances, skill multipliers, and decryptor modifiers.
- [ ] Decryptor effects on output runs, ME, TE, and invention chance.
- [ ] Manufacturing installation fee components, EIV, and system cost index.
- [ ] Structure role bonuses and rig effects by activity and security class.
- [ ] Manufacturing time modifiers and stacking behavior.
- [ ] Reaction restrictions, required skills, inputs, and eligible facilities.
- [ ] Planetary industry limits, extraction behavior, taxes, and recipes.
- [ ] Tech III relic categories, reverse engineering, and component chains.
- [ ] Market fees and applicable skill/standing effects.
- [ ] Character job capacity and current slot limits.

## Entry format

For each claim, record the proposed wording, origin, affected concept, why it matters, evidence currently available, and the test or source needed to resolve it.
